Experimental Evaluation of Patellofemoral Durability

Feb 2018 – 2019 · M.S. thesis (Bioengineering, 2019) · Published in J. Mech. Behav. Biomed. Mater. (2018)

Background & approach

Patellar fracture, wear, and loosening can lead to revision after total knee replacement, but few in vitro patellofemoral durability tests existed.

Using worst-case loading profiles derived from in vivo data of patients performing a single-leg lunge, I designed the fixtures and conducted the experimental portion of the study on a 6-degree-of-freedom VIVO joint simulator, comparing implant deformation with computational predictions.

Key findings

  • Deformation patterns on the patellar implants were distinct for each loading profile.
  • Experimental deformation correlated strongly with model predictions (overall peak 0.84 mm).
  • The loading profiles and test method can be used for pre-clinical testing of new patellar designs.

Methods & tools

AMTI VIVO joint simulator, Keyence 3D scanning, fixture design, rapid prototyping, machining
