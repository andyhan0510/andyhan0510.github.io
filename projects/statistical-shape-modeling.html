Statistical Shape Modeling of the Human Lower Limb

Aug 2020 – May 2021 · Center for Orthopaedic Biomechanics

Background & approach

Population-based simulation requires models that represent how bone shape varies between people. This project built the anatomical foundation used in later hip studies.

Key findings

  • Landmarked and quality-checked hundreds of CT-derived lower-limb bone models.
  • Automated coordinate-system alignment for the pelvis, femur, tibia, and patella in MATLAB.
  • Generated statistical shape models of individual bones and multi-bone lower-limb configurations to characterize anatomical variability.

Methods & tools

MATLAB, landmark extraction, registration, principal component analysis
