Research Experience

  • Iliopsoas Contact and Excursion After Virtual Total Hip Arthroplasty (May 2026 – Nov 2026)
    Center for Orthopaedic Biomechanics, University of Denver · Faculty advisor: Chadd Clary
    • Integrated CT-based iliopsoas finite element models, anatomy-constrained cup placement, and an automated femoral implantation algorithm to simulate extension and gait in 130 virtually reconstructed hips.
    • Quantified contact occurrence, instantaneous and cumulative contact area, and musculotendinous excursion at pelvic and implant interfaces.
    • Evaluated anatomical and implant associations and compared three femoral configurations.
  • Automated Acetabular Cup Planning and Native Anatomy Restoration (Auto-Cup) (Jan 2026 – Nov 2026)
    Center for Orthopaedic Biomechanics, University of Denver · Faculty advisor: Chadd Clary
    • Developed a CT-based framework to map anatomically feasible cup-center regions and select cup size and pose under containment and bone-coverage constraints.
    • Evaluated the framework against experimentally implanted cups in 24 cadaveric hips using postoperative optical scans.
    • Applied the framework to 200 native hips to quantify trade-offs in hip-center restoration, host-bone coverage, bone removal, and anterior cup prominence.
  • Native Iliopsoas-Bone Contact and Excursion Across Hip Postures (Dec 2023 – Nov 2026)
    Center for Orthopaedic Biomechanics, University of Denver · Faculty advisor: Chadd Clary
    • Developed a semi-automated CT-based finite element workflow for native peri-acetabular iliopsoas-bone interaction in 200 hips with subject-specific anatomy and regional passive musculotendinous properties.
    • Established a population-level reference for contact location, accumulated contact footprint, and direction-resolved tissue excursion during extension and axial rotation.
    • Identified motion-specific contact and excursion patterns and explored associations with 28 anatomical variables.
  • Extra-Articular Bony Impingement in Total Hip Arthroplasty (May 2021 – Dec 2023)
    Center for Orthopaedic Biomechanics, University of Denver · Faculty advisor: Chadd Clary
    • Developed CT-based 3D bone-on-bone collision models in 200 hips to quantify extra-articular osseous clearance across hip motion and prescribed pelvic tilt.
    • Identified anatomical correlates of standing external-rotation and seated internal-rotation clearance using LASSO, multivariable regression, and nested cross-validation.
    • Characterized posture-dependent morphology–clearance relationships, establishing an anatomical basis for subsequent implant and soft-tissue modeling.
  • Statistical Shape Modeling of the Human Lower Limb (Aug 2020 – May 2021)
    Center for Orthopaedic Biomechanics, University of Denver · Faculty advisor: Chadd Clary
    • Landmarked and quality-checked hundreds of CT-derived lower-limb bone models and automated coordinate alignment of the pelvis, femur, tibia, and patella in MATLAB.
    • Generated statistical shape models of individual bones and multi-bone configurations to characterize anatomical variability.
  • Patellofemoral Implant Mechanics and Experimental Validation (Feb 2018 – Aug 2020)
    Center for Orthopaedic Biomechanics, University of Denver · Faculty advisor: Chadd Clary
    • Designed and fabricated fixtures for AMTI VIVO simulator testing of patellofemoral implants, including durability testing and single-leg-lunge kinematics.
    • Measured cementless patellar implant micromotion using optical tracking and GOM Aramis digital image correlation, and compared measurements with finite element predictions.
    • Used Keyence 3D scanning, rapid prototyping, machining, and computational analysis to evaluate implant deformation and fixation mechanics.
